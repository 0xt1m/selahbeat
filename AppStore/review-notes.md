
1. Purpose and target audience
SelahBeat is a metronome for drummers and musicians who play in church worship
teams.

The problem it solves: a worship drummer plays a different set of songs every
week, each at a specific tempo, and has to start the click at exactly the right
moment in a live service. General-purpose metronome apps make you re-enter a
tempo every time, and most take a noticeable moment to begin sounding after you
press start — which is disruptive if you have to stop and restart mid-song.

SelahBeat keeps its audio engine running continuously so the first click sounds
within a few milliseconds of pressing start, lets you save a service (a set list)
with the tempo, time signature and key for each song, and remembers every song
you have used so it comes straight back next week.

Target audience: volunteer and professional musicians in church worship teams,
primarily drummers. It is equally usable as a general-purpose metronome.

2. Setting up and accessing the main features
No account, login, or credentials of any kind are required. There is nothing
to sign up for and no paid content. Every feature is available immediately on
first launch.

To exercise the app:

1. Metronome tab — press START. The click begins immediately. Type a tempo by
   tapping the large number, drag the tempo slider, or press TAP repeatedly to
   tap a tempo in. The Mix section sets the relative volume of the accent,
   quarter, eighth and sixteenth notes.
2. Services tab — tap "New Service" to create a set list, then "Add Song".
   Search for a song (for example "Way Maker"), which shows results from your own
   library and from our song catalog. Add it, then drag to reorder, and set a key
   or a service-specific tempo.
3. Library tab — every song you have added is saved here and can be edited,
   duplicated or deleted.

The app is fully functional with no network connection. A connection is used only
to download the shared song catalog described below.

3. External services, tools and platforms

- selahbeat.com — a server we operate ourselves. It serves a read-only
  catalog of song titles, artists, tempos, time signatures and keys, so a user
  can look up a tempo they do not know. The app downloads this catalog and
  caches it locally so lookup continues to work offline. This is a one-way
  download; the app sends no user data to it and there is no account associated
  with it.

That is the complete list. The app contains:

- no authentication or account system
- no analytics, tracking, advertising or attribution SDKs
- no payment processing or in-app purchases
- no AI or machine-learning services
- no third-party SDKs of any kind
- no user-generated content, and therefore no reporting or blocking mechanism

All audio is generated procedurally on the device; the app ships no audio files.

4. Regional differences

There are none. The app behaves identically in every region and offers the same
features worldwide. It is currently localised in English only. No content is
region-restricted, and nothing changes based on the user's location — the app
does not request or use location data.

5. Regulated industry and third-party material

SelahBeat does not operate in a regulated industry.

Regarding third-party material: the song catalog contains only factual metadata —
song title, artist name, tempo in beats per minute, time signature and musical
key. It contains no copyrighted creative content: no audio recordings, no
lyrics, no sheet music, no chord charts and no album artwork. Artist names appear
solely to identify which song is meant, in the same way a setlist or a library
catalogue entry does.

The only sound the app produces is a synthesised metronome click generated
mathematically on the device at runtime.
